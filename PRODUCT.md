# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Friends and like-minded people. The blog is not for traffic or conversion — it's a quiet corner where the author shares life experiences and technical reflections with a small circle. Readers come from links shared in conversations, not search engines. They read in relaxed moments: late evenings, weekends, with a cup of tea. They value depth over speed.

## Product Purpose

A personal writing space that feels like a handwritten letter, not a broadcast. Success is measured by whether a friend says "I read your thing, it was nice" — not by pageviews. The blog exists because writing clarifies thinking, and sharing builds connection.

## Brand Personality

**Quiet, restrained, thoughtful.**

Three words that describe how this space should feel when you land on it:
- **Quiet** — no popups, no banners, no "subscribe" CTAs, no noise. The page breathes.
- **Restrained** — typography and whitespace do the work. Color and decoration are intentional, never filler.
- **Thoughtful** — the writing rewards patience. The design should too: comfortable line length, gentle rhythm, no rush.

## Anti-references

- **Tech blog template**: dark mode default, neon accents, purple-to-blue gradients, hero sections with terminal mockups, "10x developer" energy. This blog is not a tech portfolio.
- **SaaS landing page**: hero + 3 feature cards + CTA button, gradient backgrounds, "boost your productivity" copy, conversion-optimized layouts.
- **AI slop patterns**: glassmorphism, nested cards, gradient text, identical card grids, numbered section markers (01/02/03), tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows above every heading.

## Design Principles

1. **Content is the page.** The article IS the design. Navigation, metadata, and chrome exist only to serve the reading experience.
2. **Typography before decoration.** Hierarchy comes from font size, weight, and spacing — not from colored boxes, borders, or icons.
3. **Empty space is intentional.** Generous margins and padding are a design choice, not a waste. The page should feel open, not cramped.
4. **Restrained color.** Neutral body, dark text, one accent used sparingly (links, focus states, subtle highlights). No decorative color.
5. **Familiar but not templated.** The reading experience should feel comfortable and expected — like a well-typeset book — but not like every other blog on the internet.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA compliance: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: no forced animations
- Semantic HTML: proper heading hierarchy, landmark regions, keyboard navigation
- `text-wrap: balance` on headings for even line distribution
